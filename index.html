For Vanessa

My Love,

Thank you for spending the last year with me. It has been unspeakably wonderful. My love for you is so great that, at times, it becomes difficult to put into words. However, from the moment we met, and especially once I got to know you, I have respected and appreciated you greatly. Even when we were “just friends,” I loved you immensely, and as time goes on, it seems I continue to love you more and more each day.

There is not one morning when I do not wake up thinking about you, nor one night when I fall asleep without you at the center of my mind. You are, and have been, a big reason I get up in the morning and keep pushing myself to become a better person. There are so many parts of you for which I am thankful, but I am especially thankful for your patience and kindness.

Happy 1 year, my love. I can’t wait for the many more years to come.
